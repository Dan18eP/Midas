# Impeccable Design Skill

A rigorous design framework that elevates frontend interfaces from generic AI generation to world-class craft and production-grade engineering.

## Core Design Laws

1. **The Brief Wins:** Always honor pinned aesthetics, materials, brand colors, and domain requirements. Never overwrite the client's established palette with generic presets.
2. **Refinement Preserves; Redesign Replaces:**
   - In pure chromatic refinement, preserve all layout, JSX, spacing, component semantics, and business logic.
   - Only alter color values, tokens, and visual contrast.
3. **No AI Slop:**
   - Ban generic purple-to-blue gradients.
   - Ban uncalibrated spacing, nested cards with identical borders, and arbitrary box shadows.
   - Tint shadows toward the background hue instead of pure black at low opacity.
4. **Color & Contrast Discipline:**
   - Maintain strict separation between Brand Identity (gold, cream, bronze, slate) and Semantic Health States (emerald for healthy, amber for degraded/quarantine, rose for critical error).
   - Ensure primary text against base surfaces exceeds WCAG AAA (14:1) and secondary text exceeds WCAG AA (4.5:1+).
5. **Optical Balance:**
   - Align baselines, badges, and status pills optically, not just with raw mathematical offsets.
   - Inner radius formula: $R_{inner} = R_{outer} - gap$ when nested.
