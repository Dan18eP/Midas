# Taste Skill / UI UX Pro Max

A comprehensive aesthetic standard ensuring enterprise data applications reflect high technical elegance, calm authority, and intentional art direction.

## Core Rules for Data & Observability Dashboards

1. **Quiet Authority for Complex Workspaces:**
   - On workspaces where engineers and analysts spend hours (like Midas Active Lakehouse), visual quietness and high information density take precedence over flashy gradients.
   - Restrained borders, warm off-white alabaster backgrounds, and crisp typographic scales reduce cognitive fatigue.
2. **Elimination of Artificial "AI Tells":**
   - No glowing neon borders or rainbow charts without domain justification.
   - Clean single-source lighting and subtle border contrast (`#E8E0D0` on `#FBF9F5`).
3. **Component Polish:**
   - Compact status pills with 1.5px border or subtle tinted background (`#4D7C0F]/10`).
   - Clean tabular metric boxes (`#F4F1EA`) with muted headers (`#4B4639`) and prominent figures (`#1C1E21`).
4. **Pre-Flight Design Validation:**
   - Verify readability in both compact viewport and full screen.
   - Ensure interactive states (hover, focus-visible, active) are distinct and follow the brand accent (`#B8860B`).
